# Research

Curated, published research behind archy's design decisions: the literature survey and the empirical studies that gate which metrics ship, get deferred, or get rejected. These are the version-controlled, citable counterparts to raw working notes; they are referenced from the [README](../../README.md), [`ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md), and [`FUTURE.md`](../FUTURE.md).

The catalogue that informs the roadmap. |

| [`ACYCLICITY_DILUTION_EMPIRICS.md`](ACYCLICITY_DILUTION_EMPIRICS.md) | The #192 decision: should `overall` be changed so a single cycle registers at scale? Corpus study of count-sensitive acyclicity terms; rejected (they penalize large near-acyclic codebases and duplicate the FP-free `cycles.added` signal). The dilution is documented as intended. Harness in `bench/acyclicity_dilution.py`. |
| [`TYPE_HINT_COVERAGE_EMPIRICS.md`](TYPE_HINT_COVERAGE_EMPIRICS.md) | The study that rejected type-hint coverage in both axis and diagnostic form. |
| [`AUTONOMY_CONTINUUM_SYNTHESIS.md`](AUTONOMY_CONTINUUM_SYNTHESIS.md) | Synthesis of Tracy Bannon's AI Autonomy Continuum talk: where archy sits on the continuum, the productivity-theater / score-gaming risk, and autonomy-tiered gating (deterministic checks may block, inferential checks stay advisory). Positioning and vocabulary, not new metrics. |
| [`AGENT_CAUSAL_REASONING_SYNTHESIS.md`](AGENT_CAUSAL_REASONING_SYNTHESIS.md) | Synthesis of Phroneses' "Agents Cannot Maintain Systems" (additive vs transformative gap), tested against the 2025-2026 causal-reasoning, fitness-function, and AI-PR-review literature. Frames archy as an externalized causal model; maps the article's five causal dimensions to archy's surface (gap analysis); proposes `archy_simulate` (counterfactual pre-edit check) for agent causal reasoning and a per-PR structural review brief for human judgment. Positioning and design input, not new metrics. |
| [`PREWALK_READ_REDUCTION_SYNTHESIS.md`](PREWALK_READ_REDUCTION_SYNTHESIS.md) | Synthesis of Stencil's "Prewalk" (the agent bill is `O(reads)`, not `O(edits)`) against archy's big question (#289): can an archy structural pre-edit brief reduce the exploratory reads an agent does before editing? Concludes prewalk's warm-trajectory handoff is not something a tool-mediated artifact can replicate (archy is weak for the handoff analogue), leaving only the substitution claim, which archy already half-ships (`invariant_brief`, `impact`, `graph(focus)`) but had never been measured. **Outcome: a documented non-result.** The #259 harness's arm-C extension ran to N=22 (`claude-sonnet-5`, flask); the brief did not reliably reduce reads-before-first-edit (pre_edit_reads median Δ −2.0, 14/8 pairs, sign p=0.286, after an N=10 hint at p=0.109 that did not survive more data). **NO-GO on any `archy brief` surface, now on evidence**, not an unproven prior (see `bench/agent_footprint_results.md`). |
| [`PATTERN_DETECTION_EMPIRICS.md`](PATTERN_DETECTION_EMPIRICS.md) | The #288 decision: can archy *infer* Cosmic Python's architectural patterns (Hexagonal, Repository, Service Layer, Message Bus, UoW/Aggregate/CQRS/DI) from the dependency graph? **NO-GO on evidence.** Three inferred detectors over three corpora (cosmicpython branches / framework-coupled controls / general projects): purity-based is tautological (0 flags across 31 repos), position-based separates the corpora perfectly at repo level but has **0.00 domain-identification precision on every control** (it flags CLI entrypoints and exception modules, never `models.py`) plus a 20% false-positive rate on projects where the question does not apply, and naming-based is a convention check that is 65% vacuous and misses transitive coupling. Meanwhile a 12-line `.importlinter` contract with `include_external_packages = True` catches the hardest control case with full chains and line numbers. **Contracts delta is negative**; the general reason is that "domain" is a statement of intent and the import graph does not carry intent. Harness in `bench/pattern_detection.py`. |
| [`CONSTRAINT_AWARE_ORCHESTRATION_SYNTHESIS.md`](CONSTRAINT_AWARE_ORCHESTRATION_SYNTHESIS.md) | Answer to #298: should archy ship agent-framework integrations (LangGraph, Pydantic AI, CrewAI, OpenAI Agents SDK) to serve the DevDigest/Constraint-Decay call for "constraint-aware orchestration"? Finding: the gap the article names is **archy-side, not integration-side.** All four frameworks are already MCP clients and the remaining guard glue is 10-30 lines each, while the advisory-vs-hard-failure dial the article asks for is only half-built (`sdp.mode: warn\|error` proves the mechanism but is scoped to one built-in heuristic that is off by default; project-authored `forbid` rules have no severity at all). **Native adapters wontfix; docs recipe deferred behind a usage signal (zero inbound demand today); the gate bench not scheduled on cost** (its obvious endpoint is tautological, but three bounded-retry endpoints and a held-out-constraint design are not, all recorded). Two independently-justified archy-side items fall out: per-rule severity (#309) and a canned remediation string (#310). |
| [`INLOOP_PREVALENCE_EMPIRICS.md`](INLOOP_PREVALENCE_EMPIRICS.md) | Empirical answer to the synthesis's deepest open question. Replays 1,072 human-authored commits across 11 mature repos to measure how often a real change introduces a structural regression archy catches. Finding: new import cycles are rare per commit (0.5%) but concentrate in large changes (median 7x the files) and are severe when they fire; the composite score drops on 29% of commits but 98% of drops are sub-0.005 noise (validating the advisory-vs-blocking split). Reframes archy as a rare-firing, low-FP gate; specifies the agent A/B protocol for the remaining causal question. Harness in `bench/inloop_prevalence.py`. |
