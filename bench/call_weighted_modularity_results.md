## Weight policy used

Per the script docstring: edges with `call_count > 0` are weighted by that count; import-only edges fall back to weight=1. This preserves every structural edge in the modularity computation; the call counts amplify the already-counted edges rather than zeroing out the structural ones.

Two alternatives were considered:

- All-edges-weight-zero-when-no-calls. Collapses plugin/registry shapes (starlette, scrapy) to trivial Q because most edges become weight=0. Structural information is real and shouldn't be discarded.
- `call_count` directly with missing-as-zero. Same problem with bigger surface (most non-scientific projects have many import-only edges).
